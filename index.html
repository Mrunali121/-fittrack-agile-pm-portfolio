Decision spotlight

Saying no to a good idea, on purpose

On Day 6 of Sprint 2, the Head of Product asked for a "streak freeze" feature based on early beta feedback — a reasonable, well-intentioned request.

Instead of quietly absorbing it, I assessed the impact against remaining capacity and brought the sponsor two real options: add it and risk the sprint goal, or defer it. We deferred it to Sprint 3.

Result: the sprint goal held, the feature still shipped one sprint later, and the burndown recovered cleanly — visible in the chart in the repo.
