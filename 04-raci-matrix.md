# RACI Matrix — FitTrack MVP

**R** = Responsible · **A** = Accountable · **C** = Consulted · **I** = Informed

| Deliverable / Activity | PM | Eng Lead | Design Lead | QA Lead | Sponsor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project charter | R | C | C | I | A |
| Product backlog prioritization | A | C | C | I | R |
| Sprint planning | R | A | C | C | I |
| Architecture / technical decisions | I | A/R | I | C | I |
| UI/UX design | C | I | A/R | I | I |
| Feature development | I | A | C | I | I |
| QA test plan & execution | I | C | I | A/R | I |
| Risk register maintenance | A/R | C | I | C | I |
| Sprint review demo | R | C | C | C | A |
| Release go/no-go decision | R | C | I | C | A |
| Beta launch communication | A/R | I | I | I | C |
| Stakeholder status reporting | A/R | I | I | I | I |

## Notes
- The PM is Accountable for status reporting and risk register upkeep — these are
  process artifacts, not technical deliverables, so ownership sits with the PM rather
  than being distributed.
- Release go/no-go is Responsible with the PM, Accountable with the Sponsor — the PM
  runs the process and makes the recommendation, but the Sponsor holds final sign-off
  given business risk (beta cohort reputation).
