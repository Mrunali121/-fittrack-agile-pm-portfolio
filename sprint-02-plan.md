## Mid-Sprint Change: Scope Creep Incident
On Day 6, the Head of Product requested an additional feature — a "streak freeze"
(let users protect a streak once per week) — for the beta launch, based on early
user testing feedback.

**PM decision process:**
1. Assessed impact: new story estimated at 5 points, team had 3 days of slack
   remaining based on early burndown trend.
2. Did **not** silently absorb it — brought it to the sponsor with two options:
   (a) add it and accept schedule risk on FT-9, or (b) defer to Sprint 3.
3. Sponsor agreed to defer to Sprint 3 backlog (added as FT-10 follow-up), protecting
   the sprint goal.

This decision and its impact on the burndown are visualized in
[`06-sprint-burndown-chart.png`](../06-sprint-burndown-chart.png).

All 4 committed stories delivered on time. Sprint goal met. Streak-freeze feature
correctly deferred rather than causing a late-sprint scramble.
